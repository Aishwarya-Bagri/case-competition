From one factory to a cluster

FactoryOS is the decision layer. It reads from the meters and machines the factory already has, and sits above Schneider EcoStruxure rather than competing with it.

Phase 1: one factory

1 plant meter, 3 to 5 machine meters, a production-order upload. Weeks, not months.

Phase 2: ten factories

The model learns machine patterns, energy intensity per product and common inefficiencies across plants.

Phase 3: SME clusters

Pithampur, Noida, Rajkot, Coimbatore, Pune, Surat. Cluster-level benchmarks and shared demand planning.
